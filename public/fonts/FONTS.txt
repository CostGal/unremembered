Fonts shipped with the game (all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL.txt):

- Pixelify Sans (PixelifySans-Regular-latin.woff2)
  Copyright 2021 The Pixelify Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans)
- Handjet (Handjet-Regular-latin.woff2, Handjet-Regular-greek.woff2)
  Copyright 2022 The Handjet Project Authors (https://github.com/rosettatype/handjet)
- Tiny5 (Tiny5-Regular-latin.woff2, Tiny5-Regular-greek.woff2)
  Copyright 2023 The Tiny5 Project Authors (https://github.com/Gissio/font_tiny5)

The subsets are the ones served by Google Fonts (latin, plus greek for the
fonts that have it). Pixelify Sans is the English default. Greek (Settings >
Language) uses Handjet, as the "Handjet UI" face in index.html: Handjet's
letters with Pixelify Sans digits. Tiny5 is optional (Settings > Font).
